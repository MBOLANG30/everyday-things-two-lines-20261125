Rain explained to a 10-year-old in two lines: the sky wrings out a giant sponge it filled from the sea, and an honest second line says what that sponge maps — clouds gathering and releasing water as drops — and what it leaves out — no real sponge or hand, just invisible vapor rising and cold air making it heavy enough to fall. A single-purpose Node program that writes the two lines to entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
